When and where will Pillayar Nonbu 2026 be held?
Pillayar Nonbu 2026 will be held on Saturday, December 19th, 2026, in the San Francisco Bay Area. Specific venue address and event details are shared directly with registered families prior to the event.
When is Thirukarthigai & Thirukalyanam 2026 at Concord Temple?
Following community tradition, Pillayar Nonbu 2026 organizers are coordinating Thirukarthigai & Thirukalyanam at The Shiva Murugan Temple (1803 2nd St, Concord, CA 94519) on Sunday, November 29th, 2026. All devotees, families, and friends are warmly invited to participate. Offerings of flowers for pooja are welcome.
Who are the organizers of the Pillayar Nonbu 2026 event?
Each year, a new committee of six host families are selected by random draw during Pillayar Nonbu to organize the following year's event. Families who have previously organized are excluded from the draw to maximize community participation — as tracked by NCNA database records dating back to 1991.
How can new families in the Bay Area register or get involved?
New or relocating Nagarathar families can reach out to pillayarnonbu2k26@gmail.com to get added to local WhatsApp groups and the long-standing community database (https://ab.encna.org). Once added to the database, families can update their contact details and family photo.
Why is there no printed address book for PN 2026?
  • Keeping Registration Fees Low: Printing physical address books in India and shipping them over costs around ~$3,500 USD (₹325,000 INR). Skipping printed books keeps registration fees affordable for families and eases the organizers' workload as they focus their time and effort on this year's food preparation.
  • Official NCNA Digital Address Book: It is a 100% free app for all community members—with platform costs fully borne by NCNA as a service to our community. The organizing team is working closely with NCNA on this initiative for an improved experience of the digital address book at https://ab.encna.org. This allows families to keep their photo and contact details up-to-date in real time. Stay tuned for more info!
  • Your Privacy Comes First: Built safely and hosted through a secure paid platform, the app follows strict security standards, and is thoroughly reviewed by our community software engineers. Your contact details and photo will only be displayed to other members after you give your explicit consent.
Is there a Pillayar Nonbu fund used by organizers for event expenses?
  • 100% Self-Funded Event: The event is entirely self-funded through attendee registration fees, with the six host families pooling initial personal funds to get event planning started.
  • Responsible Budgeting & Surplus Rollover: We budget responsibly to avoid collecting surplus funds. If any remaining balance is left at the end of the event, 100% of it is transferred to the subsequent year's organizers.
  • Full Transparency & No External Funding: No organizational funding or reimbursements are involved for Pillayar Nonbu 2026. The organizers are fully committed to complete financial transparency for this event.

How were venue options evaluated for Pillayar Nonbu 2026?
  • Selected Venue: A familiar, centrally located Bay Area facility was secured at $9,100 through community negotiations—much lower compared to previous years.
  • Evaluation & Comparisons: The organizers evaluated multiple Bay Area options (temples, banquet halls, event centers, and schools). Alternatives ranged significantly in price (quotes reaching up to $23,100+) or had strict restrictions like early 7:00 PM cutoffs and 100% upfront payment demands.
  • Full Transparency: Specific venue names and detailed cost comparison spreadsheets are available upon request to the organizers.
What event costs do the organizers cover personally?
  • Personal Attire: Hosting Pillayar Nonbu is a deeply meaningful honor for the six host families. To celebrate this togetherness, the families personally fund their matching traditional attire (Achis' sarees and Annans' veshti/shirts) out of pocket.
  • Thirukarthigai & Thirukalyanam: By community tradition, the six host families personally sponsor 100% of the Thirukarthigai & Thirukalyanam event at Concord Temple—covering all pooja materials, prasadam, and temple charges.
  • Heartfelt Return Gifts: In the spirit of Nagarathar hospitality, the host families personally fund a memorable return gift and goodie bag for every attending family, ensuring no guest walks away without a token of our appreciation.
What food changes are coming for Pillayar Nonbu 2026?

Over the years, our community food models have evolved—moving from individual home potluck assignments (pre-2023) to full vendor catering (2023–2025). While catering offered convenience, rapidly rising commercial costs for 2026 would steeply increase attendee registration fees.

Community Survey Preference (192 Responses):

  • Option C: Hybrid Model (Assigned + Bulk) — 43.8% (Selected Model)
  • Option B: 100% Catered Food — 28.1%
  • Option A: 100% Volunteer Assigned — 28.1%

Based on feedback from the last community survey, the organizers chose Option C being the community preference to keep costs to the minimum while preserving cultural traditions.

What is the food menu?

An easy to prepare menu has been put together with thoughts from organizers and volunteers stepping forward to support the hybrid food model and keeping traditions alive and meaningful.

  • Home Prepared: Elai Maavu, Vellai Paniyaram, Kandharappam, Medhu Vadai, Modhagam (Modhakam)
  • Bulk Cooking: Idly, Sambhar, Tomato Chutney, Ven Pongal
  • Other Supplies: Coffee / Tea, Cookies (Kids) / Mixture / Juice (Kids)
How can one volunteer for food preparation?
  • Option 1: Small Group Prep (Home-Based): Groups of Aachis meet in local Bay Area neighborhoods to prepare items like Kandharappam, Vellai Paniyaram, Medhu Vadai, or Modhagam. You can also volunteer to prepare and bring Elai Maavu.
  • Option 2: Commercial Bulk Cooking & Prep: Preparing bulk items (Idli, Sambar, Chutney, Pongal) using commercial stoves in 2–3 hour shifts at our designated kitchen facility.
  • Pre-Event Prep Option: You can also volunteer to chop vegetables at home on the evening before the event (pick up vegetables, chop, and bring them over).
Why should I volunteer?
  • Food volunteers receive a full discount on the food portion of their registration fee. 🎉
  • Kitchen volunteer spots are capped, so early signup is highly encouraged! ⚡
  • It is so much fun to come & cook together, meet people, and support the community! 🤝

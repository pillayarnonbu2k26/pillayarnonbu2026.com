When and where will Pillayar Nonbu 2026 be held?
Pillayar Nonbu 2026 will be held on Saturday, December 19, 2026, at the India Community Center (ICC) in Milpitas, CA (525 Los Coches St).
When is Thirukarthigai & Thirukalyanam 2026 at Concord Temple?
Following community tradition, Northern California Pillayar Nonbu 2026 organizers are coordinating Thirukarthigai & Thirukalyanam at The Shiva Murugan Temple (1803 2nd St, Concord, CA 94519) on Sunday, November 29, 2026. All devotees, families, and friends are warmly invited to participate. Offerings of flowers for pooja are welcome.
Who are the organizers of the Pillayar Nonbu 2026 event?
  • Each year, a new set of six host families is selected by random draw during Pillayar Nonbu to organize the subsequent year's event.
  • Families who have previously organized are excluded from the draw to maximize community participation—a rule tracked through historical NCNA database records dating back to 1991.
  • Following the January 3, 2026 draw, a final committee of six host families was confirmed to organize Pillayar Nonbu 2026.
How can new families in the Bay Area register or get involved?
New or relocating Nagarathar families can reach out to pillayarnonbu2k26@gmail.com to get added to local WhatsApp groups and the long-standing community database (https://ab.encna.org). Once added to the database, families can update their contact details and family photo.
Why is there no printed address book for PN 2026?
  • Overseas printing, tariffs, and shipping costs have increased significantly. Accommodating this major expense alongside bulk food preparation creates significant budget challenges.
  • We're moving to a more sustainable model by transitioning to NCNA’s official digital address book initiative (web and mobile app), allowing families to keep their profile updated securely (https://ab.encna.org).
  • Built with paid service partners, the app will adhere to industry security standards verified by software engineering professionals. Explicit consent will be required from every member before contact details and photos are displayed.
Is there a central Pillayar Nonbu fund used by organizers for event expenses?
  • The event is completely non-profit and 100% self-funded through attendee registration fees, with the six host families pooling initial personal funds independently.
  • Organizers budget responsibly to avoid collecting surplus funds, and any remaining balance at the end of the event is fully transferred to the subsequent year's host committee.
  • No organizational funding or reimbursements are accepted or used for Pillayar Nonbu 2026.
What is the total estimated budget for Pillayar Nonbu 2026?
Detailed budget breakdowns and registration fees will be published by the host committee.
How is the registration cost per family calculated?
Registration costs are calculated based on fixed venue costs, food, and supply expenses divided across expected attending families.
How were venue options evaluated for Pillayar Nonbu 2026?
  • ICC Milpitas was selected at $9,100 (saving $1,472 compared to 2025) with community negotiations and given community familiarity.
  • Other options evaluated included Livermore Temple ($8,000 quote with a 7 PM cutoff and 100% upfront payment), Royal Palace Fremont ($23,106 quote), Concord Banquet Hall, Oyasis, Sunnyvale Temple, and school facilities.
What event costs do the host organizers cover personally?
Host organizers pool initial seed funds and volunteer personal time to execute event logistics.
What food changes are coming for Pillayar Nonbu 2026?
Over the years, our community food models have evolved—moving from individual home potluck assignments (pre-2023) to full vendor catering (2023–2025). While catering offered convenience, rapidly rising commercial costs for 2026 would steeply increase attendee registration fees. Based on 192 responses to the community survey, 43.8% preferred Option C (Hybrid Model: Assigned + Bulk Cooking), 28.1% preferred Option B (100% Catered), and 28.1% preferred Option A (100% Volunteer Assigned). Option C was chosen to keep costs low while preserving cultural traditions.
What is the food menu?

An easy to prepare menu has been put together with thoughts from organizers and volunteers stepping forward to support the hybrid food model:

  • Home Prepared: Elai Maavu, Vellai Paniyaram, Kandharappam, Medhu Vadai, Modhagam (Modhakam)
  • Bulk Cooking: Idly, Sambhar, Tomato Chutney, Ven Pongal
  • Other Supplies: Coffee / Tea, Cookies (Kids) / Mixture / Juice (Kids)
How can one volunteer for food preparation?
  • Option 1 (Small Group Prep - Home-Based): Groups of 10–12 Aachis meet in neighborhoods (e.g. Cupertino/Fremont/Dublin area) to prepare items like Kandharappam, Vellai Paniyaram, Medhu Vadai, Modhagam, or Elai Maavu.
  • Option 2 (Commercial Bulk Cooking & Prep): Preparing bulk items (Idli, Sambar, Chutney, Pongal) using commercial stoves in 2–3 hour shifts at our designated kitchen facility, or chopping vegetables at home the evening before.
Why should I volunteer?
  • Food volunteers receive a full discount on the food portion of their registration fee. 🎉
  • Kitchen volunteer spots are capped, so early signup is highly encouraged! ⚡
  • It is so much fun to come & cook together, meet people, and support the community! 🤝
How does the Silver Yelam (Auction) sponsorship work?
Families can sponsor silver auction articles—either by donating items with personal family significance or sponsoring specific ritual items (such as Kuthu Vilakku)—to bridge budget gaps anonymously while preserving tradition.
What is the policy for soliciting ritual and temple item sponsorships?
Organizers must clarify requirements with donors to accept only items they willingly offer to sponsor, avoiding steering or forcing donors into purchasing expensive items out of pocket.

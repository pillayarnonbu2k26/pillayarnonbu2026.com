Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to public event questions and private organizer guidelines.

What is Pillayar Nonbu?

Pillayar Nonbu is a sacred festival celebrated annually by the Nattukottai Nagarathar (Chettinad) community, honoring Lord Pillayar (Ganesha) with prayers, fasting, and community gathering.

When and where is Pillayar Nonbu 2026 being held?

Pillayar Nonbu 2026 will take place on Saturday, December 19, 2026 from 2:00 PM to 9:30 PM in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Who can attend the event?

The main gathering is by invitation for Bay Area Nagarathar families and registered community members. For details or invitation inquiries, please contact pillayarnonbu2k26@gmail.com.

What is the traditional dress code?

Traditional Indian attire is requested for all attendees (Veshti/Shirt for men and Saree/Salwar for women).

How can I get regular updates about the event?

Event announcements and schedules are published on this website and sent out via our community email newsletters.

🔒 Private / Internal Guidelines

These guidelines are intended for organizing host families, volunteers, and registered attendees.

How does the Yelam (Auction) process work?

The Yelam item collection and bidding proceeds directly support community causes (NSNA & NCNA funds). Items should be tagged and submitted to the auction committee prior to the start of the puja.

What are the host family setup responsibilities?

Host families should arrive by 12:30 PM to set up puja items, seating arrangements, and food service stations before doors open at 2:00 PM.

How are food prep and prasadam distributed?

All food prep items are managed by the food committee. Prasadam and Nonbu Elai distribution will follow the puja rituals around 7:30 PM.

Who do I contact for venue access & emergency support?

Host leads and committee members can reach the logistics team at pillayarnonbu2k26@gmail.com or via the internal organizing chat group.

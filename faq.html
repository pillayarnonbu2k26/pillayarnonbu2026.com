Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Pillayar Nonbu 2026.

What is Pillayar Nonbu?

Pillayar Nonbu is a sacred festival celebrated annually by the Nattukottai Nagarathar (Chettinad) community, honoring Lord Pillayar (Ganesha) with prayers, fasting, and community gathering.

When and where is Pillayar Nonbu 2026 being held?

Pillayar Nonbu 2026 will take place on Saturday, December 19, 2026 from 2:00 PM to 9:30 PM in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Who can attend the event?

The main gathering is by invitation for Bay Area Nagarathar families and registered community members. For details or invitation inquiries, please contact pillayarnonbu2k26@gmail.com.

What is the traditional dress code?

Traditional Indian attire is requested for all attendees (Veshti/Shirt for men and Saree/Salwar for women).

How can I get regular updates about the event?

Event announcements and schedules are published on this website and sent out via our community email newsletters.
